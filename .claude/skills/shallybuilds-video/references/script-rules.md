# Script rules

Built from Shally's own "SHALLYBUILDS CREATIVE ENGINE" and "Viral Script Formula" docs (Google Drive).

## Length and shape
- **≤ 105 spoken words ≈ 40 seconds** (at ~2.7 words/sec). Check with `script_to_captions.py`. The last word must land by 41 s.
- Shape: **HOOK (0–3 s) → BODY (value) → CTA (last ~5 s)**.
- **The hook must be different every time. The body pattern and CTA can repeat.** Write 3 hook options per script and put the chosen one first.

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
One emotional, scroll-stopping line. Pick one type:
- **Bold claim with proof on screen** ("If you have a phone and data, you have everything you need…")
- **Callout + pain** ("If you've posted 30 videos and still have 200 views, this is why.")
- **Trending hook**: anchor to something everyone is talking about now (verified news, a viral moment).
- **Question they can't ignore** ("Why are Nigerians quietly earning in dollars with AI thumbnails?")

## Body frameworks (pick one per script)
- **AIDA**: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action.
- **PAS**: name the Problem, Agitate it, give the Solution.
- **PASTOR**: Problem, Amplify, Story, Testimony (real only), Offer, Response.
- **Emotional anchor**: tie the outcome to someone they love ("so your parents don't have to struggle").
- **Wake-up ("gaslighting") technique**: make them question their delay without shaming ("How many tomorrows has it been?").
- **Value bomb / list**: 3 numbered items, each with a concrete action.

## CTA
- Standard: "The full [thing] is in the link in my bio. Grab it before you scroll." Vary the noun, keep the shape.
- Never "comment X and I'll DM you" (that's the competitors' pattern; ours is link in bio).

## Originality rules
1. Research gives **patterns** only: topic, format, length, hook *type*. Never reuse a competitor's sentence, joke, example or list order.
2. Use our own examples (Nigerian context, phone-first, naira where it fits) and our own angle.
3. `originality_check.py` must print `ORIGINAL ✓` (no 6-word run shared with any source, ≤ 5 % shared 4-word phrases).
4. No fake quotes, fake screenshots, invented stats, or "X said" claims without a link to where they said it.

## Voice checklist
- Short sentences. Contractions. "You" more than "I".
- One Pidgin punchline at most.
- Numbers as digits on screen, words in speech when clearer.
- Read it aloud: if you run out of breath, split the sentence.
