# GRWM Edit Studio: the 5 styles

`index.html` is the editor. A user adds at least 2 clips plus a sound or voiceover, picks one of the styles below, and the page edits and records a 9:16 video in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Each style copies one reference video from the shared Drive folder. The timings below come from frame-by-frame cut detection and beat analysis of those videos. The TikTok end card was left out of every measurement.

| Style | Reference video | Sound | Pace |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zip, Snap & Slay** | GRWM 4 WORK/ASMR | Song plus loud ASMR sounds | Cut on every beat (117 BPM, about 0.5s) |
| **Honey Glow** | GRWM 4 WORK/song | Song | Jump cut every 2 beats (about 0.7–1.3s) |
| **Mirror Talk** | SIMPLE GRWM WITH VOICEOVER | Voiceover, optional music bed | Long takes of 4–6s, cut on speech pauses |
| **Spill The Day** | Dodo Pizza outing vlog | Voiceover, optional music bed | Cut about every 0.9s |
| **Golden Layers** | GRWM TEMPLATE (CapCut) | Song | Footage at 1.6×, cut every 2 beats (117 BPM) |

The occasion (Church, Work or Outing) changes the title text and the label on the final clip ("At church.", "At work.", "Out & about!").

## 1. Zip, Snap & Slay

**What the reference does**
- **0–2.9s, the hook:** a wide shot talking to the camera in a robe. You hear the clip's own sound and the song plays quietly. A white caption ("Wtf is she doing?!*") pops in word by word on a wavy, slightly tilted baseline.
- **2.9–3.8s, the title:** a hard cut to a face close-up. A huge hot-pink condensed title (GRWM / FOR / WORK!) fills the frame top to bottom in three stretched lines.
- **3.8s, the beat drop:** cut to the wide shot. The title stays for about 4 beats. From here, cuts land on every beat (0.33–0.5s), with some half-beat double cuts.
- **Labels:** a white tag with a thin pointer line sits at the top right next to the head. It reads "Hair", "Dress Up", "Fit Check", "En route!" or "At work.". The outline appears first, then the text wipes in from the right.
- **Sound:** loud, broadband ASMR hits on the cuts (fabric, zip, spray, clicks) over the song.

**How the editor builds it**
- Clip 1 becomes the hook (with its own audio) and then a 1.65× punch-in close-up under the title.
- At the drop: a white flash, a bass drop and a whoosh going into it.
- Every other clip gets a window of beats with a cut on every beat and a 22% chance of a half-beat split.
- Each clip's moment plays its ASMR sound at full level at the start of its window, and again halfway through windows longer than 2.6s. Soft fabric swishes land on about a quarter of the other cuts. The song dips under every sound so it cuts through.

## 2. Honey Glow

**What the reference does:** a makeup GRWM filmed in one spot. A white handwritten "Grwm" sits over a small italic "for work", top centre, for the first 3 seconds. Gentle jump cuts every 0.4–1.9s run through skincare, lips, hair and perfume, ending on a held smile and wave. The look is warm, glowy skin. The song runs throughout.

**How the editor builds it:** a script title that fades in and out, jump cuts every 2 beats with a slow 1.0→1.035 push-in, and a warm soft-light grade with a soft glow. The last shot is held for about 2 seconds, with a sparkle and a short fade at the end. ASMR sounds are quiet (55%).

## 3. Mirror Talk

**What the reference does:** a calm, motivational voiceover over long takes (moisturiser, dressing, mirror selfie, walking outside). There's no title. Gold bold captions show one word at a time, top centre.

**How the editor builds it:** the voiceover drives the length. Windows are spread across the clips, with jump cuts every 3.6–5s placed on the nearest pause in the speech. The caption words come from the script box and are timed to the voice the editor detects. Background music plays at 20% and dips to 8% while you speak. ASMR is off by default.

## 4. Spill The Day

**What the reference does:** a story-time event vlog. A bold white serif title with a drop shadow sits at the top for the first 2 seconds. Hard cuts every 0.8–1.0s for 67 seconds straight, mixing selfie talk and b-roll. Sentence subtitles in a small white sans with a shadow sit at the lower middle and follow the voiceover.

**How the editor builds it:** cuts about every 0.9s (locked to the background music's beat when there is music), alternating push-in and pull-out zooms, a punchier grade, and the serif title plus sentence-chunk subtitles (up to 7 words, 2 lines).

## 5. Golden Layers

**What the reference does:** outfit build-up in one spot (robe, jeans, tank, vest, bag, sunglasses), sped up so each change pops. Jump cuts land on the beat, with selfie close-up inserts. A gold condensed serif "GRWM" types in letter by letter, a gold script "For the day" wipes in, and a small tracked line reads "OUTFIT DIARIES | OOTD | FASHION". The last frame cuts back to the first look, so the video loops.

**How the editor builds it:** 1.6× footage, cuts every 2 beats (4 beats when the song is fast), a 1.07→1.0 punch on each cut, and a 1.75× close-up on the third shot of each clip. A film grade lifts the blacks and adds a warm vignette. The final 0.45s goes back to the opening frame. A fabric swish plays on each clip change, plus the moment's ASMR sound.

## ASMR sound pack

All 18 sounds are generated in the browser, so there are no copyrighted samples or downloads. Each clip's **moment** picks its sound:

| Moment | Sound | Moment | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before / hook | Record scratch | Shoes | Heels |
| Skincare | Pump | Jewellery | Jewellery jingle |
| Makeup | Brush tap | Perfume | Two sprays |
| Lips | Lipstick pop | Bag | Clasp |
| Hair | Hairspray | Bible / notebook | Page flip |
| Getting dressed | Zip | Fit check | Camera click |
| Heading out | Car keys | Arrived | Sparkle |

There are also extra hits for transitions: fabric swish, snap, bass drop and whoosh.

## About the reference sounds

The reference videos use trending songs, which are copyrighted, so the editor doesn't include them. Users add their own song or voiceover. If you want to post with the exact trending sound, add it inside TikTok or Instagram when you post. If the trending sound has a similar tempo to the one you edited with, the cuts will still roughly line up. The Zip, Snap & Slay and Golden Layers references both run at 117 BPM.

## Limits

- The video records in real time, so a 30-second edit takes about 30 seconds. Keep the tab open while it records.
- The editor saves MP4 where the browser can (Safari, recent Chrome) and WebM elsewhere.
- Speech detection works best on a clean voiceover. If the voiceover already has music in it, captions are spread evenly over the clip.
- iPhone HEVC clips may not open in Chrome on Windows. Exporting them as MP4 (H.264) fixes this.
