## Her video editing style

Based on scene-by-scene analysis of the shared video plus her two biggest videos.

### 1. Camera and set
- **Talking head, mid close-up, direct eye contact**, holding a **mini wireless lapel mic** in her hand. The visible mic is part of the look.
- **Clean, neutral home studio**: plain grey or off-white wall, a plant or shelf, background slightly blurred.
- **Soft key light** from the front with visible catchlights in her eyes. No harsh shadows.
- **Wardrobe**: soft pastel knits or plain tees, gold jewellery, polished hair and nails.

### 2. The hook (0–3s)
She shows the result or the problem within 2 seconds, before any explanation.
- Photoshoot video: the finished AI photos appear on screen while she says the first line.
- Humanizer video: a "96% AI" detector score flashes while she says "you can get caught".
- Opus video: a **topic title card at the top** ("5 ways to make money with Opus 5.5") plus pop-up social-proof posts.

### 3. Layouts she rotates between
| Layout | When she uses it |
|---|---|
| **Split screen on a dark grid background.** Demo or screen recording on top, her face in a rounded frame below | Step-by-step tutorials |
| **Green screen**: her cut-out over a screen recording | Showing a tool while talking |
| **Full-screen screen recording** with a bold black title bar naming the tool | Walking through each tool |
| **Floating Mac-style window cards** (red/yellow/green dots) popping up above her head | Newer, more premium style |
| **Full-screen final result** | Just before the CTA |

### 4. Pacing
- **Jump cuts on every breath.** There is no dead air.
- **Something changes every 1–2 seconds** (a cut, a graphic, or new text). Tool walkthroughs stretch to 4–8 seconds.
- **Small 5–10% zoom-ins** on cut points and whenever a new list number appears.

### 5. Captions
- **Current style**: an **elegant serif font** (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display look), white with a soft drop shadow, lower third, appearing phrase by phrase, with **key words in bold italics**.
- **Older style**: a rounded sans-serif in a **translucent black pill** with the active word highlighted **pink**.

### 6. Graphics
- **A big number** (1, 2, 3…) plus a **pill label** at the start of each list item.
- **Green "success" pills** such as "✓ Done in 11 min", "40% cheaper" and "✓ Lip synced".
- **Step cards** stacked vertically ("1 Find one → 2 Strong sample → 3 Full launch video").
- **Crossed-out text** for the wrong idea, then the right one underneath.
- **Big red X** over the problem, and **sparkle overlays** on AI results.
- Small emoji icons (📩 🔥 💎 ⏳) inside the pills.

### 7. Sound
- A **quiet lo-fi or tech beat** underneath the whole video.
- **Pops, whooshes and UI clicks** when graphics appear, and a **ding** on green check pills.
- Her voice is crisp and close to the mic, with no echo.

### 8. CTA ending
- Always **"Comment [WORD] and I'll send you the guide."** This is comment-to-DM automation. It gets her 2–3K comments, which pushes the videos to more people.
- Newer videos animate a **comment box typing the keyword**, followed by a "📩 Guide sent to your DMs" pill.
- The caption repeats "Guide in comments".

### How to copy this style (tools)
- **CapCut**: auto captions (choose a serif template), split-screen layout, keyframed zoom-ins, remove background (green-screen effect), and its sound-effect library.
- **Any lapel mic** (DJI Mic, Hollyland Lark, or a cheaper option) held in your hand for the look.
- **One soft light** (ring light or softbox) in front of you, with a plain wall behind.
- **Canva or Figma** to make reusable pill badges, number cards and a dark grid background.
- **A comment-to-DM tool** (e.g. ManyChat), or reply to DMs by hand at first.
