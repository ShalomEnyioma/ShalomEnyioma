# Editing Style Guide: "Faceless Videos on Autopilot" reel

Source: [instagram.com/p/DbokeaVArAt](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbokeaVArAt/) · 50s · analysed 2026-09-30 with vidIQ
Topic: a promo for FacelessReels.com, an AI tool that makes and auto-posts faceless videos.

## The style in one line
**The presenter stays in the bottom third. Above them sits a dark grid "stage" where clean, animated app mockups float in, with a red headline pill that changes for each new point.**

## What makes it different from a normal tutorial reel
1. **No raw screen recordings.** Every app screen is a **clean recreated mockup**: rounded cards, dark mode, no browser clutter.
2. **The presenter stays in the bottom third** while graphics fill the top. You always see a face and the proof on screen together.
3. **Proof is animated, not static.** Video thumbnails float with view counts on them, and cards connect with dotted lines.
4. **A red headline pill for each section** works like chapter titles, so viewers always know where they are.

## Style spec

### Layout (two modes)
| Mode | Share of the video | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Stage mode** | ~60% | Dark charcoal grid background (`#121212`). Presenter cropped into the **bottom third**. Floating UI cards, thumbnail grids or diagrams in the top two thirds. Red header pill at the very top. |
| **B. A-roll mode** | ~40% | Full-frame talking head, close-up, podcast mic in the foreground. Captions only. Used for emotional or persuasive lines. |

It switches between modes every 3–5 seconds.

### Hook (0–2s)
- **A bold claim:** "This is the best tool in the world for making faceless content…"
- The **red headline pill** ("Faceless Videos On Full Autopilot") is on screen from the first frame.
- UI cards already **moving**, and a **riser + swoosh** sound.
- By 0:02, a **3×3 grid of animated video thumbnails** appears. That's the proof.

### Pacing
- A new visual every **1.8–2.5 seconds**.
- **Punch-in zooms** on the punchlines during A-roll.
- Cards **float** with slow keyframed drift, so they never sit completely still.

### Captions
- **Bold sans-serif** (Inter or Montserrat Bold), white with a black stroke or shadow.
- **Centred, lower-middle**, popping in **word by word**.
- **Key words highlighted yellow or purple.**

### Colour palette
| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Background grid | Charcoal `#121212` |
| Headline pills | Red `#E50914` with white text |
| Accents, tags, CTA, URL | Purple `#7C3AED` |
| Text | White `#FFFFFF` |
| Highlights on winning items | Green |

The presenter footage is warm: warm skin tones and a wood-slat backdrop, which contrasts nicely with the cold dark UI.

### Graphics
- Rounded **dark-mode app cards** (dashboard, onboarding steps, scheduling screen).
- A **3×3 thumbnail grid** and a row of **viral video thumbnails with view counts**, with the best ones outlined in green.
- A **diagram**: video card → dotted line → your card, with purple tags `[HOOK]` `[PACING]` `[TEXT STYLE]`.
- A **phone mockup** of a profile with big numbers ("30 days from now…").
- **End card**: landing page plus a **big purple URL**.

### Sound
- A **low tech-synth beat** under the whole video.
- A **riser + swoosh** on the hook, **sub-bass hits** on big screen changes.
- **Pops and mouse clicks** when cards appear, and a **ping** when view counts show.
- A **cash-register pop** on the "results" moment, and a **rising tone + click** on the CTA.

### Set and look
- A **podcast mic on a boom arm** (SM7B-style) in shot.
- A **wood-slat wall**, soft key light, warm tones.
- Casual outfit: cap and a plain tee.

### CTA
- Ends on the tool's landing page with the **URL large in purple**, looping back to the first screen so replays feel seamless.

## How to recreate it (CapCut workflow)

**Set up once (make a reusable template):**
1. In **Canva or Figma**, make a 1080×1920 **dark grid background**, a **red pill header** (editable text), **purple tag pills**, and a **rounded dark card frame**. Export as PNGs.
2. Make **app mockups**: screenshot the tool, then place the screenshot inside a rounded card with a soft shadow. Skip the browser bar and tabs.
3. Save a CapCut project with the background, header pill and a masked "bottom-third presenter" slot. Duplicate it for every new video.

**For each video:**
1. **Film** a close-up with your mic in shot, warm light and a clean background. Leave headroom so you can crop to the bottom third.
2. **Rough cut**: remove pauses with CapCut's "Remove silences" or jump cuts.
3. **Stage sections**: put the grid background on the main track, your video as an overlay → **Mask (rectangle, rounded)** → move it to the bottom third.
4. **Floating cards**: add mockup PNGs as overlays. Keyframe **scale 90→100% + fade in**, then a slow 2–3% drift.
5. **Header pill**: a text overlay on a red rounded rectangle, changed at every new point.
6. **A-roll sections**: go full frame, and keyframe a **110–115% punch-in** on punchlines.
7. **Captions**: Auto captions → choose a **bold, word-by-word highlight** preset → set highlight colour to yellow or purple.
8. **Sound**: a low synth beat at ~15–20% volume, plus SFX from CapCut's library ("whoosh", "pop", "click", "riser", "bass hit", "cash").
9. **End card**: landing page mockup plus a big purple URL or CTA word.

**Faster or pro options:** **Submagic** for one-click word-highlight captions. **After Effects** for 3D-perspective floating cards and dotted-line connectors. **Figma** for pixel-perfect UI mockups.
